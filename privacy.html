Privacy Policy for Morning Faith

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Morning Faith offers both device-only features and optional social features. What we collect depends on which you use.

Local-only data: Your alarm times and daily scripture verse progress are stored entirely on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.

Account data (if you create an account to use social features): If you choose to sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple, we collect your display name, a unique account identifier, and (depending on the sign-in method and your settings) your email address, in order to create and manage your account.

Social and activity data: If you use friends, leaderboards, or challenges, we collect and store: your display name, the time it takes you to complete a verse (verse completion time), your streaks, your challenge progress, badges you've earned, and your friend connections (who you've sent, received, or accepted friend requests from).

2. How Your Information Is Shared

Your display name, streaks, verse completion times, challenge progress, and badges may be visible to users you have added as friends, including on shared leaderboards. We do not make this information visible to the general public or to users who are not your confirmed friends. We do not sell your data to third parties, and we do not use your data for advertising.

3. Blocking and Reporting

Morning Faith allows you to block other users. Blocking another user prevents them from sending you friend requests, seeing your profile or activity, or appearing on leaderboards visible to you, and prevents you from appearing to them in the same way. You can unblock a user at any time from your Blocked Users list in Settings. You can also report a user directly within the app; reports are reviewed by us to help keep the community safe.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Morning Faith's social features:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, and backend hosting) — stores your account and social/activity data as described above. See Supabase's privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google Sign-In — used only if you choose to sign in with Google. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Sign in with Apple — used only if you choose to sign in with Apple. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

If you purchase any premium features, we use official Apple In-App Purchases to process those payments. Apple manages this payment data securely under their own privacy policy. We do not have access to your credit card information or banking details.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Account and social/activity data is retained for as long as your account exists. You may delete your account at any time from within the app, under Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, friend connections, verse completion history, streaks, challenge progress, and badges from our servers. Local, device-only data (alarms, non-social scripture progress) is unaffected by account deletion and can be cleared separately by deleting the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Morning Faith's social features (accounts, friends, leaderboards) are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect account data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app's features evolve. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last Updated" date, and where appropriate, communicated within the app.
